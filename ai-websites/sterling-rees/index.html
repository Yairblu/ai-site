Specialist Expertise

Moving between
Gibraltar, Spain & the UK?

Relocating across these three jurisdictions is more complex than it might appear. Gibraltar largely follows UK tax legislation — but Spain operates an entirely different system, with its own rules on residency, foreign assets, and income from abroad.

Getting the transition right matters from day one. Whether you're an individual establishing residency, a business extending your footprint, or navigating obligations in more than one jurisdiction simultaneously — we know this territory well.

We advise on the tax implications of your move, help you meet your obligations in each jurisdiction, and make sure nothing falls through the gaps between systems.

🇬🇮

Gibraltar

Broadly follows UK tax legislation. Favourable environment for individuals and international businesses, with its own distinct rules on residency and income.

🇪🇸

Spain

An entirely separate system. Residency triggers distinct obligations — including on worldwide income and foreign assets — that require careful planning.

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

Ongoing UK tax obligations often follow individuals and businesses after a move. We coordinate Gibraltar and UK compliance so nothing is missed.
