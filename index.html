About Us

Established practice.
Direct partner involvement.

Drummonds was founded in Gibraltar in 1991 by Hugh Drummond. For over three decades the firm has served local and international clients from its office on Main Street, offering the kind of direct, considered advice that a large multi-tier practice cannot readily provide.

Hugh Drummond works personally with each client. Matters are handled at partner level rather than delegated down a management structure — a distinction that matters considerably when the issues at stake are complex, time-sensitive, or require coordinated advice across jurisdictions.

The firm maintains a close working relationship with Haysmacintyre & Co, a leading London-based accountancy firm, providing clients with seamless access to UK expertise alongside Gibraltar-based services.

Hugh Drummond — Founder & Principal

With practice established in Gibraltar since 1991, Hugh Drummond brings deep familiarity with the regulatory and tax environment in the territory, as well as extensive experience in cross-border matters involving the United Kingdom.

Clients work directly with Hugh throughout their engagement — from initial instruction to completion — ensuring continuity, accountability, and a consistently high standard of advice.

MSI Global Alliance Drummonds is a member of MSI Global Alliance, an international network of independent accounting and law firms, providing clients with access to professional advice in jurisdictions worldwide.
What We Do

Our Services

Accounting for Local & Offshore Companies and Trusts

Preparation of accounts, bookkeeping, and financial reporting for Gibraltar-based companies, international business companies, and trust structures. Delivered with the precision and confidentiality that these vehicles require.

Insurance & Regulatory Compliance

Compliance support for regulated entities operating in Gibraltar's financial services sector, including insurance companies and those subject to oversight by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

UK Company Tax Compliance Services

Corporation tax returns, computations, and ongoing compliance for UK-registered companies. Working in close coordination with Haysmacintyre & Co in London, we provide thorough and timely UK tax compliance for clients with UK corporate interests.

UK Taxation for High-Net-Worth Individuals

Personal tax compliance and advisory services for high-net-worth individuals with UK tax obligations — including self-assessment returns, residence and domicile considerations, and coordinated advice across Gibraltar and UK jurisdictions.
